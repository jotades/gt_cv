I am applying for the data role at Bending Spoons. I am an R\&D engineer and PhD candidate at the University of Bologna, with more than five years at CNR-STIIMA, the Italian National Research Council, and I am Head of Product and co-founder of Rehabilia Technologies. Across that work the common thread is making data trustworthy enough to base decisions on.

For OmniTrack, an indoor navigation system for blind users, I own the data path end to end: UWB ranging and IMU time series from wearable ESP32 tags, position estimation in ROS 2, session logging in MongoDB, and analysis in Python (pandas, Jupyter). I designed OmniAnalytics, a tool that turns raw recordings from 16 participants into trial metrics such as route similarity (DTW, Fr\'echet distance), speed and target discovery. The rule I set for it is the one I would bring to your reporting systems: raw data is never modified, and every correction, exclusion and annotation is stored separately and traceable.

At Rehabilia the data serves business decisions. I run research with clinics and education centres on their needs, the tools they use and how they make spending and purchasing decisions, and I combine it with study data (our device PhiCube reached a usability score of 85.8/100 with 19 children and is in two multicentre studies with 134 patients) to set priorities. I use Claude and Claude Code every day to move faster in analysis and prototyping, and I hold Anthropic's certifications for both.

My data work so far has been at research scale rather than on production pipelines serving millions of users, and that is the gap I want to close. I learn quickly, I hold myself to a high bar on correctness, and I live near Milan, so spending the first months in your office is easy for me.

I would welcome the chance to take your tests and discuss the role further.
