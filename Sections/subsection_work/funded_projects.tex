{National and foundation-funded health programmes}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \href{https://www.fit4medrob.it/}{\color{blue}Fit4MedRob} (MUR, national programme): developed and deployed PhiCube devices in Italian clinics, defining hardware/software requirements with clinicians through user-centred design.
    \item \href{https://www.fondazionecariplo.it/it/progetti/ricerca/progetto-crew.html}{\color{blue}Giocabile} (Fondazione Cariplo): designed the user interface and a serious game for children with cerebral palsy on the PhiCube platform.
    \item V.I.T.A.: IoT multisensory feedback system within a VR/AR therapy platform for autism.
    \item \href{https://www.stiima.cnr.it/progetti-ricerca/riprto/}{\color{blue}Rip@rto and Rientr@} (INAIL): driving and wheelchair VR simulators built in Unity.
\end{itemize}
