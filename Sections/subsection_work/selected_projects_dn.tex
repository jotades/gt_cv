{WashB: ultrasonic teeth-cleaning device (MSc thesis, Politecnico di Milano)}
{}
{}
{}
{
\begin{itemize}
\item Researched brushing, flossing, materials and users' oral-care habits, and identified ultrasound as a way to remove biofilm in hard-to-reach areas.
\item Designed a device that works regardless of manual dexterity and adapts to different dental arch shapes; selected materials and optimised the shape of its functional components.
\end{itemize}
